How CampusPass works

Four simple steps. No training needed.

1

Request filed

A nurse, teacher, or parent submits a movement request with the reason for early dismissal.

2

Admin approves

The school administrator reviews the request and either approves or rejects it instantly.

3

Guard verifies

At the gate, the guard scans the RFID card or looks up the student manually. Only approved students leave.

4

Return tracked

When the student returns, a second scan logs them back in and the class teacher is notified.

Everything your school needs

A complete system for student safety and movement control.

RFID scanning

Students tap their card at the gate. The system verifies approval in real time — no paper slips needed.

Manual fallback

If the scanner fails, the guard can look up any student by ID, name, or RFID tag and process them manually.

Teacher alerts

Class teachers receive instant notifications when a student in their class leaves campus or returns.

Bulk import

Upload an entire class roster from Excel in one click. Download templates, import, done.

Live attendance

The roster shows real-time present/absent status for every learner. Downloadable at any time.

Uganda curriculum

Classes from P1 to P7 and S1 to S6 are built right in. No configuration required.

A role for everyone on campus

Every user has a tailored experience based on their responsibilities.

Super Admin

  • Approves or removes school registrations
  • Oversees the entire platform
  • Manages the list of active campuses

School Admin

  • Creates staff accounts (teachers, nurses, guards, parents)
  • Views the full learner roster (class teachers register learners)
  • Approves or rejects movement requests
  • Downloads reports and clears resolved requests

Teacher

  • Assigned to one class (e.g. P3 or S2)
  • Registers and imports learners for their class
  • Views class roster with live present/absent status
  • Receives notifications when students leave or return

Nurse

  • Files medical exit requests for sick students
  • Provides the reason directly in the system
  • Tracks the status of every request they submit

Guard

  • Verifies students at the gate via RFID scan
  • Uses the manual gate panel when the scanner fails
  • Signs students out and back in with full logging

Parent

  • Linked to their child in the system
  • Can be contacted when a student is released
  • Provides an extra layer of accountability
