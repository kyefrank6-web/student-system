Gate RFID scanner

Scan or type an RFID tag to sign a learner out (with an approved request) or in when they return. Their photo and details appear after each scan.

Manual lookup
Scan field

USB scanners usually send Enter after the tag — the scan runs automatically.

How it works

Sign OUT — learner has an approved exit request and is on campus.

Sign IN — learner was previously signed out and is returning (approval closes).

The learner's profile photo and ID are shown on screen immediately after a successful scan.
